[32m│[0m ## 2. Rules                                                                                       [32m│[0m
[32m│[0m                                                                                                   [32m│[0m
[32m│[0m 1. Every converted expression is an f-string. No percent operator, no `str` formatting method,    [32m│[0m
[32m│[0m    no string concatenation.                                                                       [32m│[0m
[32m│[0m 2. The rendered output must be byte-for-byte what it was. The tests assert on it.                 [32m│[0m
[32m│[0m 3. Width and precision specifiers carry over into the format spec: `%-20s` becomes `:<20`,        [32m│[0m
[32m│[0m    `%10s` becomes `:>10`, `%.2f` becomes `:.2f`, `%3d` becomes `:3d`, `%03d` becomes `:03d`.      [32m│[0m
[32m│[0m 4. A literal percent sign is `%%` in a `%`-format string and a single `%` in an f-string.         [32m│[0m
[32m│[0m    `shop/tax/rates.py` and `shop/legacy_invoice.py` both publish percentages this way and both    [32m│[0m
[32m│[0m    are easy to get wrong.                                                                         [32m│[0m
[32m│[0m 5. Do not rename anything, do not reorder functions, do not add or remove behaviour, do not       [32m│[0m
[32m│[0m    introduce a helper to do the formatting.                                                       [32m│[0m
[32m│[0m 6. Keep every line within 100 columns; the project's ruff config enforces it.                     [32m│[0m
[32m│[0m                                                                                                   [32m│[0m
[32m│[0m ## 3. Worked examples                                                                             [32m│[0m
